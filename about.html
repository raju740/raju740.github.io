About Me

My story is built on transition, pressure, and steady growth.

I did not enter this field from the easiest path. What makes my journey meaningful is the way challenge changed me, strengthened me, and helped me grow into a more focused student.

Journey

From medical background to engineering mindset

One of the most important chapters of my life was shifting from a different academic background into Computer Engineering. That transition was not simple, but it taught me how to adapt under pressure and continue learning even when the starting point was difficult.

  • I had to rebuild confidence in a completely new technical environment.
  • I learned to face unfamiliar concepts instead of stepping away from them.
  • This shift became the foundation of my resilience and seriousness.
Purpose

Why this journey matters to me

My goal is not only to earn a degree. I want to become someone who understands responsibility, solves problems with maturity, and builds a future through consistent effort instead of shortcuts.

  • I value patience because real progress takes time.
  • I value discipline because pressure becomes manageable with structure.
  • I value hope because setbacks should not decide the final result.
Three Defining Phases

The story behind my growth

My academic identity was shaped through transition, struggle, and confidence. These phases explain why I take my growth seriously today.

Phase 01 Switching fields

Entering engineering from a different background forced me to begin again from the basics and accept that progress would take patience.

Phase 02 First semester struggle

Quizzes, assignments, fear of failure, and self-doubt made the beginning emotionally hard, but they also taught me time management and self-control.

Phase 03 Confidence and direction

Step by step, small achievements helped me believe in my abilities and look toward the future with more stability and hope.

What I Carry Forward

The qualities I want to build further

University changed the way I think. It made me more reflective about my habits, goals, and the kind of person I want to become.

Patience

I understand that not every result comes quickly. The ability to continue learning with calm effort is now a part of my mindset.

Responsibility

Deadlines, study pressure, and real academic workload taught me to become more organized and more answerable for my own progress.

Self-belief

I no longer see challenge only as fear. I now see it as something that can shape confidence when handled with discipline and consistency.
