Carefree College Life and Hidden Reality

A reflection on comfort, limited pressure, and the moment I realized that a much harder phase was waiting ahead.

My college life was enjoyable and relatively relaxed. I used to attend classes only three days a week, which left me with a lot of free time and very little academic stress. At that stage, I was more focused on enjoying life than thinking seriously about the demands of higher studies. Everything looked simple, and I felt as if this routine would continue without major difficulty. Slowly, however, that mindset began to change. As my intermediate studies came to an end, I started realizing that university life would be very different. The thought of admission, future responsibilities, and career planning created pressure that I had not felt before. That period made me understand an important truth: the comfort I was enjoying was temporary. The real struggle had not yet started, and I would soon need much more seriousness, discipline, and mental preparation to move forward.

Key Points

Highlights

  • Enjoyed a relaxed routine with limited study pressure
  • Attended college only a few days a week
  • Did not fully realize future academic challenges
