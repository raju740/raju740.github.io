Admission Journey: Confusion and Struggle

An honest account of rejection, uncertainty, and the emotional pressure that came before finding direction.

Before my final result was announced, I applied to different universities with high hopes and a very positive mindset. Unfortunately, things did not turn out the way I had expected. My entry test scores were not strong enough, and I could not secure admission in some of the universities I had dreamed about. This phase brought confusion, disappointment, and a deep sense of uncertainty. I was not sure what decision would be right for me or whether I would be able to continue my studies in an institution that matched my expectations. Some options were not appealing, while others were completely closed because of rejection. As a result, I started doubting my abilities and became mentally disturbed about the future. Even though it was a painful phase, it taught me a very important lesson: life does not always move according to our plans, and sometimes we have to accept unexpected outcomes before the next opportunity appears.
