A Turning Point: Admission in UET

The end of uncertainty and the beginning of a new chapter that brought both excitement and nervousness.

After going through a stressful admission period, I finally secured admission in Computer Engineering at UET. This moment became a major turning point in my life because it finally ended the uncertainty that had been troubling me for so long. I felt happy and relieved because I had been accepted into a respected university, and that gave me hope for the future. At the same time, I was also carrying a lot of uncertainty inside me. My previous academic background was different, so stepping into a technical field felt unfamiliar and challenging. I knew this opportunity was valuable, but I also understood that it would test me in many ways. Even with those doubts, I decided to accept the challenge and move ahead with courage. Looking back, that admission was not just a seat in a university. It was the beginning of a completely new journey that pushed me to grow, adapt, and take my future much more seriously.
