Switching Fields: From Medical to Engineering

A difficult transition that forced me to start from zero and slowly build comfort in a technical world.

Moving from a medical background into Computer Engineering was one of the hardest decisions I had ever made. I entered this field without any solid background in programming or technical concepts, so the beginning felt extremely overwhelming. Almost every topic looked new, difficult, and unfamiliar. I often struggled to understand even the basics, which created frustration and self-doubt. It also took time to adjust to the new classroom environment, the style of teaching, and the overall academic expectations. At many points, I felt as if I had started my educational journey again from the very beginning. Even though the process was slow and mentally exhausting, I kept reminding myself that change always demands patience. I did not master everything quickly, but I also did not let myself walk away from the challenge. That decision to continue, even while feeling behind, helped me stay connected with my studies and gave me the courage to improve gradually.
