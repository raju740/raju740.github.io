First Semester: Fear and Self-Doubt

A semester defined by anxiety, internal pressure, and the struggle to believe in my own potential.

My first semester was filled with fear and self-doubt. I constantly felt that I might fail my subjects and disappoint myself. Every new lecture seemed difficult, and even when I tried to understand things, I still felt behind. This created emotional stress and made me feel weak from the inside. I often questioned whether I had chosen the right field and whether I was capable of continuing in such a demanding environment. My confidence became very low, and I was rarely satisfied with my performance. At the same time, however, there was still a small voice inside me that wanted to keep going. That inner hope stopped me from giving up completely. The semester became a mental battle between fear and determination. Although it was one of the hardest phases of my academic life, it also helped shape my mindset. It taught me that personal growth often begins during the moments when we feel most uncertain about ourselves.

Key Points

Highlights

  • Constant fear of failure in every subject
  • Felt emotionally stressed and demotivated
  • Struggled to believe in personal abilities
