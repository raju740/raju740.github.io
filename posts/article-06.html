Academic Pressure: Quizzes, Assignments, and Time Management

A phase where frequent quizzes and assignments taught me that discipline matters as much as effort.

During my first semester, academic pressure became even more intense because of frequent quizzes and a heavy load of assignments. It often felt as if there was no real free time left, because one task would finish and another would immediately take its place. Managing studies was already hard for me due to my weak foundation, and this constant stream of deadlines made everything more stressful. Sometimes I felt tired, mentally drained, and unable to organize my day properly. However, this difficult routine taught me one of the most important lessons of university life: time management is not optional. Without planning, it becomes almost impossible to handle quizzes, assignments, and regular study together. Slowly, I started understanding the value of making schedules, prioritizing work, and staying disciplined. Although that pressure was exhausting, it helped me become more responsible and mature. In many ways, this phase improved not just my academic habits, but also my ability to deal with pressure in a practical way.

Key Points
