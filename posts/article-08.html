First Achievement: A Confidence Booster

A modest GPA became an important reminder that effort can still produce meaningful progress.

At the end of my first semester, I achieved a GPA of around 2.7. Although this result may not look very high from the outside, it meant a great deal to me because of everything I had struggled through during that time. I had entered the field with fear, confusion, and a weak foundation, so reaching this point felt like proof that my efforts were not wasted. For the first time, I felt that I was capable of surviving the pressure and moving forward with confidence. This small achievement changed my mindset in a positive way. It gave me hope that improvement was possible and that steady effort could produce real results. More than a number, that GPA became a personal victory for me. It reminded me that growth does not always begin with outstanding success. Sometimes, a small result is enough to rebuild self-belief and create the motivation needed for the next stages of the journey.
