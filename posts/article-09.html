Second Semester: Confidence and Disruptions

A semester that started with more confidence but was interrupted by closures, online classes, and Ramadan.

In the second semester, I felt more confident than before. I had started believing that I could manage my studies better and perform with more control. However, after about a month, the routine was disturbed because universities were closed due to external issues, including fuel crisis and conflict. This break lasted around one and a half months, and it badly affected the flow I had built for myself. Online classes were not very effective because of internet problems, distractions, and reduced motivation. During the same period, Ramadan also started, which made it more challenging to maintain a steady study schedule. Managing Sehri, Iftar, and academic work together required extra energy and discipline. Even though the situation was difficult, I tried not to disconnect completely from my studies. This phase showed me that progress is not only about academic ability. It also depends on how well we stay focused when circumstances outside our control interrupt our plans.
